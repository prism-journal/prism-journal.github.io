Summary. A critical re-analysis of published data on iron-modified biochar for microplastic removal from irrigation water. I recomputed every checkable quantity from Tables 1 and 2. Nine reproduced exactly: particle mass (2.638 µg), seasonal load (2.375 kg ha−1), demand at the highest capacity (7.3 kg ha−1), husk-derived supply (440 kg ha−1), the 26.9 % attraction-window widening, both efficiency medians (96.2 and 98.0), the count and span of the twelve efficiencies, the 67× capacity spread, and all seven surface-area ratios. Section 3.2 is the strongest contribution and it holds.

Major points. (1) Section 3.4 did not use the lowest capacity it reports: 181 kg ha−1 corresponds to 13.1 mg g−1, the lowest iron-modified capacity, not the 4.82 mg g−1 minimum given in Section 3.3, at which demand exceeds supply. The revised text states the iron-modified range explicitly and reports the 4.815 mg g−1 case. (2) The deposited record contained neither data nor code. The compiled tables, parameters with sources, and a reproduction script have since been deposited. (3) Section 2.3 declared a regression Section 3.1 did not report; the revised Methods states the reason for declining to fit it.

Minor points. Section 3.1 referred to "the only other pair to lose area" when Table 1 contains exactly one such pair. Section 2.2 described Table 1 as covering five feedstocks when three entries name a grade or treatment. The peanut-shell ratio did not follow from its own two values. All corrected.

Henry Wang · submitted 28 August 2026
